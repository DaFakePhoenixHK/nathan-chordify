## 2. Bugs fixed (evidence and decoder)

1. **Pruning dropped the song's own chords (biggest effect).**
   - The cause: the 250-candidate cap ranked classes by the best margin they reach in any cell. 48 classes tie at 0 on 回家, and the loop `break`s at the first class that does not fit.
   - On 回家 it dropped B major, the tonic and top-1 in 145 of 1,086 cells. The always-kept idiom bridge B7 (V7/IV) survived. B was then decoded as B7, B5/F# or E5/B, and E as E7, which explains most of the reported "spurious 5 chords" and "triads read as 7ths".
   - Fix (`evidence.prune`): rank cell-winners first, then everything else, each tier by its soft cell count Σ_g exp(B_cls − max B); a class that doesn't fit is skipped, not a stop.
   - The soft count alone dropped passing chords: on 10,000 Reasons, Em/B was dropped and the B°7 bridge decoded over a B bass.
2. **The power-chord mapping made "5" the runner-up in every cell.**
   - `log((p_maj+p_min)/2)` costs only log 2 below a confidently major chord. lv has no power-chord class: its training chops them to "unknown".
   - Now `log(2·min(p_maj, p_min))`, i.e. "no third" needs the third to be split between major and minor.
   - lv's class-level NLL on D2 drops from 1.21 to 1.00 nats at the fitted temperature.
3. **A silent bass stem diluted lv.**
   - 回家's bass stem is silent from 0 to 110 s and after 215 s (piano only). The flat `bas` source still sat in the weighted mean with w = 2 and shrank lv's evidence to 1/3 there.
   - Now each source has a per-cell activity: for `bas`, note coverage × a level weight.
4. **Kick/riser rumble in the bass stem was read as bass notes.** On 回家 from 111 to 116.5 s, the stem holds a non-tonal G1–F#1 rumble at −50 dBFS, 30 dB under the real bass. The tracker made it one held G note, which outvoted the E–D#–C#–B walk-down (C#°7/G, B+7/G). The `bas` activity is now scaled by a soft step 24 dB below the song's own loud bass level. The interlude now reads E | B/D# | C#m7 | B | E | B/D# | C#m7 | F#.
5. **The bass-event rule relabelled real chords** (§5.12 step 1).
   - I (4 beats) next to vi7 became vi7/1 (C → Am7/C), and a 2-beat main iii next to Imaj7 became Imaj7/3 (Bm → Gmaj7/B).
   - The longer chord's tones contain the shorter one's, so "only the bass differs" was satisfied.
   - Now: passing segments ≤ 2 beats, main segments ≤ 1 beat, and always shorter than the neighbour.
6. **IV/5 was exported as 9sus4.**
   - F/G and G9sus4-without-5th share a class. The display member was scored on a root-position proxy that hid F/G's IV/5 → I prior, so it became `G:sus4(b7,9)` while the label showed "F/G".
   - Now a root-position 9sus4/13sus4 is named by its upper structure (§5.12 step 9), so the Harte and the label agree.
   - 4/5-over-5 majmin_inv: 0.65 → 0.90.
7. **Calibration over the full vocabulary.**
   - log-softmax now runs over all ~1,900 candidates, not the pruned C. They differ only by a per-cell constant, which cancels in every path comparison; only the −6 clip sees it.
   - This makes T_s independent of pruning and lets `subset()` slice any C from one build.
8. **Speed.** The prior matrix was an O(|C|²) Python loop per key and lattice (≈ 1 s).
   - It is now one transposition-invariant full-vocabulary table per mode, built once and cached in `models/derived/` under a source hash; a test checks it against `priors.prior`.
   - Decode of a D2 item: 1.1 s → 0.12 s.

## 4. Tuning (§13.5) and the shipped defaults

- **Search (`eval/tune.py`).** 96 Sobol samples, then coordinate descent, over:
  - λ_scale [0.4, 1.6]
  - w_bas [1, 3]
  - PT bonus [0, 2.5]
  - w_prior [0, 2]
  - w_short [0, 2]
- **Fixed during the search.** κ_b = 10 and the temperatures above.
- **Slider.** (a, b, c) is fitted per configuration on the passing candidates, labelled hit or miss by the short-segment matcher at majmin_inv.
- **Objective.** J of §13.5 at Detailed, with the per-stop precision penalties.
- **Validation.** Leave-one-idiom-out (LOIO): pick the configuration on 11 idioms and score the 12th.

What the search showed:
- **The decoder parameters generalise.** The folds picked the same configuration 8 of 12 times, and a near-twin otherwise. J is flat in λ_scale: 0.7435 / 0.7408 / 0.7429 at 0.49 / 0.64 / 0.79.
  - LOIO held-out metrics stay close to in-sample: sevenths_inv 0.812 vs 0.813, majmin_inv 0.876 vs 0.881, short F1 0.523 vs 0.557.
- **The slider does not generalise.** LOIO precision per stop was 0.27 / 0.48 / 0.43 (targets 0.85 / 0.70 / 0.50).
  - mini-D2 yields only ~82 passing candidates.
  - Confidence ranks them only weakly (AUC 0.65) and salience not at all (AUC 0.55, with a sign that flips between folds).
  - Hits depend on the idiom, not on the features: descending canon 7/7 and secondary dominant 9/9, against walk-up 1/8 and 4/5-over-5 2/10.
  - A Wilson lower bound made LOIO worse. The shipped slider therefore uses **confidence only** (b = 0).
- **D2 and 回家 disagree on priors.** D2's optimum switches priors mostly off (w_prior 0.28, PT bonus 0.38, J 0.731), and that loses 回家's D#7/A# → G#m twice (checklist 0.41).

## 7. Remaining weaknesses (honest list)

1. **lv's time resolution is the main limit on passing chords.**
   - The bass tracker moves on time, while lv's upper structure lags about a beat. The dominant false positive is "previous chord over the new bass": G#m/A# for F#/A#, B♭m7/C for Cm7, E/F# for F#.
   - The same lag keeps ½-beat A–P–A neighbours at the −6 clip.
   - lv barely hears augmented fifths, so B+7 is found 0/2 on 回家 and augmented passing 0/18 on D2.
   - Remedy: M5's faster sources (ACE, piano AMT onsets). The phantom rule (§5.12 step 2) also needs AMT onsets: without them it merges real passing chords whose notes lie inside the neighbours' (A7/G in royal road).
2. **The slider's per-stop precision targets are not met out of sample** (held out 0.42 / 0.43 / 0.44). Confidence ranks passing chords only weakly (AUC 0.65), and ECE fails (0.165; §3).
3. **The local key is wrong on 回家** (WP6 `theory/key.py`, not edited): G# minor 0–29 s, E major 29–73 s, C# minor 221 s–end, where the song is in B throughout.
   - The 24-state HMM (self-transition 0.995 against ~2.5 nats/beat of emission) switches after about three beats of IV-heavy chroma, and step 3's chord refinement cannot separate I from IV.
   - In the E-major reading, B → B+7 loses its I → I+ bonus and A7 → Em6 loses the borrowed-iv bonus.
   - Suggest a much stickier key HMM for worship (P_stay ≥ 0.9995 or emission T ≥ 0.1), or modulations only when step 3 confirms them.
4. **Bass (WP5, not edited).**
   - The stem is silent before ~110 s on 回家, so slash chords there come from lv's bass head alone until M5's AMT left hand.
   - The tracker's silence threshold (35 dB under the loud level) lets kick/riser bleed through as notes. It is mitigated in fusion (fix 4); suggest a harmonicity or tighter relative-level check in `notes/bass.py`.
5. **Naming inside equivalence classes.** I6 = vi7/3 (C6 = Am7/C) and similar pairs are named by lv's root mass, which lags after a change. mir_eval then scores a different root. Rising m7 (0.85 vs B1 0.89) and royal road (0.81 vs 0.86) are the idioms where B1 still wins on majmin_inv.
6. **Synthetic tuning.** The D2-optimal values (weak priors, sharp bass) do not transfer to 回家. Only ~82 passing candidates exist, so the slider fit has high variance. The checklist is a single draft-GT song, with knife-edge items (±0.08). This needs Nathan's D1 songs (M4) and B4, Chordify's own output, before anything is claimed against Chordify.
7. **Speed of a 回家 redecode (4–5 s).** Most of it is `theory.display.build` (WP6; 5.7 of 10 s under the profiler): `spelling.key_context` recomputes scale notes ~146k times. The server's redecode route also calls `display.build` again on the returned analysis. Memoising spelling and reusing the display that `pipeline.redecode` already builds would meet the "re-scan < 2 s" target.

## 8. What was built

- **`pipeline.py`**
  - `analyze` saves `cache/<key>/decode_state.v1.npz`: the final grid, bass track, first key estimate and HCDF peaks. No pickles; evidence stays in `evidence/*.npz`.
  - `redecode(key, params=None, region=None, *, write=False)` re-runs steps 3–11 in a few seconds and reproduces `analyze` exactly (174/174 chords on 回家).
  - With `region=(t0, t1)` it re-scans ± 2 bars with `fast_rescan` parameters, fixing the boundary chords, and returns new chords as `suggestion: true` passing chords.
  - Also `load_state` / `save_state` / `decode_state` / `run_from_state`.
- **`eval/`**
  - `engine_eval.py`: `prepare` analyses every item into `eval/data/cache` (≈680 MB for 72 items); the `@engine[:detail]`, `@engine-simple` (B5) and `@lv-crf` (B1) predictors; `--params` re-decodes from the cached states.
  - `calibration.py`: T fit, NLL, ECE / reliability, break-even, observed advantage, lv offset.
  - `tune.py`: Sobol + coordinate descent + LOIO + slider fit.
  - `checklist.py` and the 回家 draft GT under `eval/gt/`.
- **Tests.** `engine/tests/test_decode_m2.py` adds 10 regression tests for the fixes, the prior table and the state round trip.
- **Updates to `test_decode.py`.**
  - The brute-force tests weight paths by exp(score / POST_TEMP).
